Selected systems

Research ideas in deployed settings

Two dialogue systems and one related document agent show how I approach knowledge acquisition, grounding, planning, and evaluation in deployed settings.

01Knowledge-grounded dialogue2018–2020

Financial knowledge-graph dialogue

Before LLM-based assistants became widespread, a fixed FAQ could not cover changing questions about companies, industries, indices, and markets. We built a financial assistant that retrieved answers from organized knowledge through natural-language dialogue.

Technical challengeLarge-scale entity and relation extraction needed labeled examples, while users needed answers from a changing body of both extracted and structured financial information.

Design I ledI connected active learning for uncertain labeling decisions, weak supervision from existing knowledge, a financial knowledge graph, and dialogue flows that map interpreted requests to graph queries.

Design trade-offGraph-backed answers could draw on changing knowledge rather than a finite set of hand-written FAQ responses. Intent understanding was a distinct component from end-to-end answer quality.

Talk video: CCKS 2020 Industry Forum — Knowledge Platforms and Intelligent Writing.

02Conversational agent2025–present

Huang Xiaoxi travel agent

A consumer-facing conversational agent for open-ended travel planning in Guizhou. An itinerary has to work in the world, not just read fluently: opening hours, travel times, available services, and changing preferences all matter.

Design I ledAs overall technical lead, I combined LLM dialogue with structured destination and service data and symbolic reasoning over practical itinerary constraints.

Evaluation approachIn product development, we used human judgments of whether complete itineraries were usable, rather than treating fluent text as a sufficient result.

Public sources: People.cn on the hybrid architecture; People's Daily on the travel service.

Early product demonstration: a February 2025 side-by-side travel-planning example, shown under the prototype name “Guigui” before the Huang Xiaoxi launch. The demo retains Guizhou Tourism Digital Network branding; I led the consumer-agent architecture at Ronghui Jinxin. This illustrates one scenario, not a controlled benchmark.

A related line of work applies the same interest in grounded information and human judgment to document-intensive decision support.

03Document and decision agent2024 onward

Bank credit-analysis agents

I initiated an agent-based product line for bank credit analysis and report writing. Related products have been implemented at multiple banks, with configurations for different data sources, reporting tasks, and review workflows.

Design questionHow can an LLM analyze a specific borrower using heterogeneous bank documents and data, follow credit procedures, and produce a report that an analyst can inspect and revise?

Design I ledI combined explicit credit workflows, streamlined data integration, case-specific financial analysis, policy-informed suggestions, and a human writing and review workspace. Repeatable steps guide the agent; analysts retain control of the report.

Published client caseFor corporate due-diligence report writing, Zhongyuan Bank reported a reduction from seven days to under ten minutes.

Public sources: Zhongyuan project procurement notice; Zhongyuan Bank case in Financial Computerizing, 2025. In that publication, Rihui Ouyang described the project as one of the first successful LLM applications in commercial-bank credit.

Other selected work

Energy supply-chain risk: I designed algorithms and architecture combining company records with structured data to assess counterparty default risk. My team implemented the software in a CNOOC credit-risk project and later in a supplier-risk project within the CNPC group.

Online-market monitoring: I designed information-extraction, entity-resolution, knowledge-graph, and risk-analysis models for national online-trading monitoring work and a later related Zhejiang Fair Online initiative. Active learning prioritized uncertain examples for human labeling.

Project recognition: Xinhua and CCTV covered the national platform. Zhejiang Fair Online was one part of a broader reform practice, together with Zhejiang Takeout Online, that received a 2021 provincial Gold Award.
